The decision

recommended; Agent Base / you pick
WA-1 · the call

Build option 1: a small Go service on whatsmeow (the library behind the mautrix and Beeper WhatsApp bridges, MPL-2.0, last pushed 29 Sep) on the Mac mini, linked to your own number as a companion device. It keeps every chat and group with history, unread counts, media thumbnails and search in a local store on the mini, reconnects by itself, and exposes a token-guarded API on the tailnet only. Agent Base's node proxies it into a WhatsApp space behind a feature flag, and feeds the Rolodex last-contact and message counts (never bodies). Sends happen only when you press Send on a message you wrote or approved.

Why the mini, not the VPS: a home broadband IP looks like an ordinary WhatsApp Desktop; Contabo is a datacentre range, which WhatsApp scores as riskier. The mini also has 16 GB against the VPS's 7.9 GB with 2.6 GB already swapped. Why not the laptop (your 2 Oct idea, same IP as your phone): it sleeps and travels, so the chats would stop whenever the lid shuts; linked devices on a different home IP from the phone are normal (Desktop at home, phone abroad).

One gap I found: FileVault is off on the mini, so the message store would sit unencrypted on its disk. I will encrypt message bodies in the store with a key held in the mini's Keychain, so this does not wait on you.

04

Ban risk, plainly, and what would change the pick

What reverses it

The risk: every non-Meta WhatsApp client (whatsmeow, Baileys, OpenWA alike) breaks WhatsApp's terms, and numbers do get restricted or banned. Bans hit bulk or automated sending, new numbers, and datacentre traffic far more than a quiet linked device. Mitigations built in: the official multi-device link (same protocol as Desktop, one device, not a second login); a home IP; no bulk sends, ever; sends only on your explicit press, rate-limited to human pace (one at a time, a few seconds apart, a daily ceiling); no broadcast, no auto-replies, no adding people to groups; read receipts only when you open a chat in the app, like a normal client; profile photos fetched lazily, never crawled. Keep in mind: WhatsApp unlinks companion devices if your phone is offline for 14 days.

What would change the pick: the 24 h soak showing the mini drop off the tailnet or whatsmeow reconnect-looping (then option 3, or 2); WhatsApp shipping a protocol change whatsmeow lags on (then Baileys); or you deciding you would rather link a second number than your own.
